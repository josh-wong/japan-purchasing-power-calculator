How it’s calculated
  • The converted price uses a European Central Bank reference rate before exchange fees. The ECB usually updates its rates each working day.

    Exchange rate used: 1 USD = 159.234 JPY.

  • Divide the yen price by the income in Japan to find the percentage of pay it costs. Apply that percentage to the comparison income to get the equivalent cost.

    When you enter a higher comparison income, the same percentage adds up to a larger amount. The converted price stays the same.

  • Divide the converted price by the comparison income to see what percentage of that income the purchase would cost.

Estimates for information only, not financial advice. Exchange rates exclude card and exchange fees. Income comparisons use the amounts you enter and do not account for differences in living costs. Your inputs stay in this browser and aren’t saved.

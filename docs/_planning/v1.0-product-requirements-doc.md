# Japan Purchasing Power Calculator

**Product requirements · v1.0**

## Goal

Help visitors and residents compare what a purchase in Japan costs relative to income in Japan and income in another currency. Show the equivalent cost based on income as the main result and provide the exchange-rate conversion as a secondary result. Both incomes can be real or hypothetical.

## Core experience

1. Choose a purchase from **Try an example** or enter a price in Japanese yen. Choose a comparison currency.
2. Use the examples or enter **Japan income** in yen and **Comparison income** in the selected currency. Both amounts represent monthly take-home pay.
3. Read the **Equivalent cost based on income**. Below it, see the **Price at the exchange rate** and what percentage of each income the purchase costs.

The first visit opens with the JP¥5,000 attraction ticket preset, US dollars, and editable example incomes of JP¥250,000 and US$4,000.00 per month. Use one guidance paragraph: “Enter monthly pay after taxes and other deductions. The prefilled amounts are editable examples, not national averages.” Include “Your inputs stay in this browser and aren’t saved.” in the footer disclaimer. Changing currency carries over the yen fields and restores an amount previously entered in that currency during the visit, if any. Otherwise, prefill the comparison income with the equivalent of US$4,000, rounded up to the nearest thousand, or ten thousand for amounts of at least 100,000. **Restore defaults** restores the starting price, incomes, and USD currency and clears remembered incomes for other currencies.

Selecting a preset fills the editable price field and recalculates the results. Show “These are example prices. Actual prices vary.” directly below the **Try an example** label and above the dropdown.

Below the equivalent cost, show the share of monthly pay the purchase takes in Japan and explain the amount that same share represents with “That’s like spending US$80.00 from the comparison income.” for the default example. Use “about” for displayed positive percentages of 0.01% or more. Label the comparison-income percentage “At the converted price.” In **How it’s calculated**, explain why entering a higher comparison income raises the equivalent cost at the same percentage and how the converted price depends on the exchange rate.

The published app uses dated European Central Bank reference rates, usually refreshed each working day. The mockup requests the latest available ECB rate when opened and includes a dated ECB snapshot for offline use.

## v1.0 scope

- English-language, single-page web app for phones and desktops.
- Offer an icon button in the header that cycles through **System**, **Light**, and **Dark**. Default to **System** and remember the chosen theme in the browser.
- Publish the app on GitHub Pages and refresh its exchange-rate snapshot through GitHub Actions. Link to the [source repository](https://github.com/josh-wong/japan-purchasing-power-calculator) from a GitHub icon beside the appearance button.
- One purchase at a time, with a currency picker covering the supported European Central Bank reference-rate currencies other than JPY. JPY is the fixed purchase currency.
- Take-home pay means income after tax and payroll deductions.
- Accept numbers with or without grouping commas, including misplaced commas in the whole-number portion and allow a period for decimals in any monetary field. Show “Number format: 4,000.50 or 4000.50.” below the income fields and associate it with all numeric inputs. Show formatting and validation errors when the visitor leaves a field, and clear that field’s error when editing resumes. Continue calculating valid inputs while typing. Ignore grouping commas in the whole-number portion when calculating, and regroup valid amounts when the visitor leaves the field, such as `2500,000` becoming `2,500,000`. A period remains the decimal separator. Calculate while the visitor types and normalize valid entries when they leave the field.
- Lead with the equivalent cost. If an income is blank, prompt for it in the main result and show the converted price below when the price and exchange rate are available. Offer a short explanation of the math and a reset to the examples.
- Display familiar currency symbols and the standard number of decimal places for each currency. Use US$80.00 for USD and JP¥5,000 for JPY. Keep currency codes in the picker and field labels.
- Keep financial inputs in the browser session. The calculation requires no account or personal data collection.

A future version could add sourced income profiles, regional comparisons, or historical affordability. v1.0 models the amounts the visitor enters.

## Acceptance criteria

- **System** follows the device’s appearance, including changes while the page is open. **Light** and **Dark** override it. Reloading restores the theme when browser storage is available.
- The first result is the equivalent cost. The default inputs produce US$80.00. Below it, the dated example rate produces a converted price of US$31.40. The income percentages are 2% for the income in Japan and 0.79% for the comparison income.
- Selecting a preset fills only the price. A manually entered price that differs from the preset selects the hidden, disabled **Custom price** option. Reset restores the JP¥5,000 attraction ticket and the starting scenario.
- The converted price remains available with both income fields blank. Each income percentage appears when its inputs are available. Switching currency restores the income entered for that currency.
- Percentages above 100% are displayed in full. A zero purchase price is valid. Pay amounts must be positive.
- If an exchange rate is unavailable, show that status for the converted price. Calculate the percentage of income in Japan and the equivalent cost from the entered amounts. Each available conversion displays its source and rate date. Use “Exchange rate used” for the numeric currency pair in **How it’s calculated**.
- All monetary fields accept input such as `5,000.50`. Preserve fractional input when it affects the calculation. Calculated monetary results use the currency's standard precision, including two places for USD and whole units for JPY and KRW.
- The form and its results work at a 320 CSS-pixel viewport, with keyboard navigation, visible focus, and screen-reader labels for the fields.
